Privacy Policy

Learn how personal information is collected, used, protected, and managed.

Last updated: January 2026

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when using our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide when creating an account, registering for courses or webinars, participating in training programs, or contacting us.

This may include your name, email address, phone number, professional title, company name, and account credentials.

We may also collect learning-related information such as course progress, completion records, assessment results, feedback, and interactions with the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to provide and maintain our training services, manage user accounts, process registrations, and deliver purchased courses and learning resources.

We may use your information to communicate important service updates, course announcements, event information, and platform improvements.

We may also use relevant information to improve learning experiences, personalize content, analyze platform usage, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Data Protection

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

Access to personal information is restricted to authorized personnel and service providers who require it to perform their responsibilities.

While we take appropriate steps to safeguard information, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share necessary information with trusted service providers who help us operate and maintain the platform. These may include payment processors, cloud infrastructure providers, analytics services, email communication services, and customer support platforms.

Such information is shared only where reasonably necessary for providing services, maintaining platform operations, or meeting legal requirements.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support essential website functionality, remember preferences, understand platform usage, and improve the user experience.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may affect certain website features and functionality.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable privacy laws, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, correct inaccurate details, or request deletion of information we hold about you.

You may also have the right to withdraw consent, request a copy of your personal information in a portable format, or request restrictions on certain types of data processing.

Requests may be submitted through the available account settings or support channels. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain account records, fulfill contractual obligations, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable retention obligations.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, services, or legal requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page with the revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice of significant changes.
