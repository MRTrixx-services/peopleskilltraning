Refund & Cancellation Policy

Understand our refund eligibility, cancellation procedures, and payment policies.

Last updated: January 2026

This policy explains the conditions under which refunds and cancellations may be requested for training programs, course subscriptions, webinars, events, and consulting services.

1. Training & Course Subscriptions

Refund requests for training programs and online courses may be submitted within five working days of purchase, provided the course has not been started or completed.

Refunds may also be considered where verified technical issues prevent access to the purchased course.

Refunds are generally not available after the five-working-day period, once a course has been partially or fully completed, or once downloadable resources have been accessed.

2. Subscription Cancellation

Subscriptions may be cancelled according to the available subscription settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.

Previous subscription payments are generally non-refundable. Cancelling a subscription does not automatically entitle the user to a refund for an earlier billing period.

3. Webinars & Events

Cancellation requests submitted at least 48 hours before the scheduled event may qualify for a full refund of the registration fee.

Refunds are generally not available for cancellation requests submitted within 48 hours of the event. Where possible, registration may be transferred to a colleague if the request is submitted at least 24 hours before the event.

If an event is cancelled or rescheduled by the platform, users will be offered a full refund or credit. Approved refunds or credits will generally be processed within three business days.

4. Consulting Services

If a consulting session has not been conducted, the customer may qualify for a full refund.

Cancellation requests submitted at least 48 hours before a scheduled session will not incur a cancellation charge.

For cancellation requests submitted within 48 hours of a scheduled session, a 50% cancellation fee may apply.

Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing. Emergency situations may be reviewed individually with appropriate supporting documentation.

5. General Refund Terms

Approved refunds are generally processed within five to ten business days and issued to the original payment method used for the purchase.

Downloadable resources are generally non-refundable once accessed. Bulk purchases may be subject to separate contractual terms.

International transactions may involve currency conversion fees or other charges imposed by the payment provider. Such charges may not be refundable.

6. How to Request a Refund

Refund requests may be submitted through the available support channel or account dashboard, where available.

Please include your order number or transaction ID, product or service name, purchase date, and a brief explanation of the reason for the request.

Supporting documents or screenshots may also be requested where necessary to review the claim. Refund requests will generally be reviewed within five business days.

7. Refund Exceptions

Refund requests may be declined in cases involving fraudulent activity, chargebacks, violations of platform policies, misuse of services, or continued use of a service after a refund request has been submitted.

Exceptional circumstances may be reviewed individually based on the information and supporting documentation provided.

8. Policy Updates

We may revise this Refund & Cancellation Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page along with the updated revision date.

Continued use of our services after an updated policy becomes effective will be subject to the revised policy.
