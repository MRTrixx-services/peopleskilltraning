Shipping & Return Policy

Information about digital delivery, cancellations, and refund requests.

Last updated: January 2026

Our services are delivered digitally. This policy explains how you receive access to purchased products and how cancellations and refund requests are handled.

1. Digital Delivery Policy

Our products include online courses, webinars, downloadable resources, and digital training materials. No physical products are shipped.

Access is generally provided through your account after successful payment confirmation. Where applicable, downloadable resources will be made available through your account.

2. Delivery and Access

Digital products are intended to be available after payment confirmation. Access may depend on the product, scheduled webinar date, or any specific instructions provided during purchase.

If you experience an access issue, please check your account and purchase details. Technical access problems will be reviewed and addressed by our support team.

3. Refund Policy

Refund requests may be submitted within five working days of the purchase date. Eligible requests will be reviewed according to the product's access and usage status.

Refund eligibility generally requires that the purchased content has not been downloaded or accessed beyond any available preview.

Approved refunds are generally processed within five to ten business days. The time taken for the amount to appear in your account may depend on your payment provider.

4. Webinar Cancellations

Cancellation requests for scheduled webinars are handled according to the time remaining before the webinar begins.

Requests submitted at least 48 hours before the scheduled webinar may qualify for a full refund. Requests submitted between 24 and 48 hours before the webinar may qualify for a 50% refund.

Cancellation requests submitted within 24 hours of the scheduled webinar are generally non-refundable.

5. Subscription Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription according to the available account or subscription settings. Once cancelled, the subscription generally remains active until the end of the current billing period.

Partial refunds for unused subscription periods are generally not provided. Digital resources that have already been accessed may also be non-refundable.

6. Technical Issues

If you cannot access a purchased course, webinar, or digital resource, the issue will be reviewed to determine whether it is related to your account, payment, browser, device, or our platform.

You may try clearing your browser cache, using another supported browser, checking your internet connection, or signing in again.

If the issue continues, submit a support request with your order details and a description of the problem.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, submit a request through the available support channel or your account dashboard, where available.

Include your order number, product name, purchase date, and a brief explanation of the reason for your request. Additional information may be requested to review the request.

Each request will be reviewed, and approved refunds will be processed within the applicable processing period.

8. Refund Exceptions

Refund requests may be declined where digital content has already been downloaded or accessed beyond the permitted preview, where the request is submitted outside the applicable refund period, or where there is evidence of fraudulent activity or a violation of our terms.

Exceptional circumstances, including verified technical access problems, may be reviewed individually.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Shipping & Return Policy from time to time. Any revised version will be published on this page along with its updated date.

Your continued use of our services after the revised policy becomes effective will be subject to the updated terms.
